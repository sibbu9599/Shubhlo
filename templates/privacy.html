🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Security

Your privacy and event security are our highest priority at Shubhlo.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Shubhlo to book wedding venues, caterers, or event vendors, we collect minimal required details to process your reservations smoothly:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, and email address for account authentication and OTP verification.
  • Event Details: Event date, location/tehsil, guest count, and selected vendor service categories.
  • Vendor Application Details: Business name, service offerings, pricing, GST numbers (if applicable), and operational phone numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for:

  • Generating verified booking passes, receipts, and order confirmation status.
  • Connecting customers with approved vendors for seamless service delivery.
  • Sending critical real-time SMS notifications regarding booking updates or payment confirmations.
  • Ensuring platform integrity and preventing unauthorized duplicate registrations.

3. Data Protection & Security

We enforce strict access controls and encrypted database protocols. Shubhlo never sells or rents user or vendor personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Account Deletion & Control

Users and vendors maintain full rights over their data. You may request account deletion or data clearance directly through account settings or by contacting our support team at support@shubhlo.com.
